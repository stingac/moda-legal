Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

What we collect. The name you give, the name you give your GOAT, your answers to the three opening questions, your phone's time zone, your limits and lock hours, the sessions you start, how many times you unlocked or went back to work, your level, and for each finished day whether you stayed under your limit. We also keep a short log of which steps of the app you reached, including which plan you picked. If you add an email, we store it so you can sign in.

What we do not collect. Which apps you block, how many minutes you spend in any app, and anything you do inside an app. That information stays on your phone. Apple's Screen Time shows it to you on this phone only and it is never sent to us.

Where it is kept. On your phone, and in our database (Supabase) under an account that belongs to you.

Who we share it with. Nobody. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it. Until you delete your account. You can do that in the app, under settings. It removes everything we hold about you.

Your data, your call. Delete account in the app's settings removes everything we hold about you, at once. To stop the lock, switch off Screen Time access for the app in the Settings app.
